### [童一枝筆──兒童文學精選2](https://www.macaudata.mo/books/detail?bno=b001999)
[湯梅笑](https://www.macaudata.mo/authordetail?id=8420788d6ec34dcd83cb9ffbd012ad68&view=mutil); [廖子馨](https://www.macaudata.mo/authordetail?id=2aace1449420415f9a94bc7137a6b569&view=mutil); [鄧曉炯](https://www.macaudata.mo/authordetail?id=ff52de14214841508fe3838852265d30&view=mutil); [盧傑樺](https://www.macaudata.mo/authordetail?id=d27b7beaa9d2413cba0485d968e28373&view=mutil) | [澳門筆會](https://www.macaudata.mo/mechanism/detailed?id=cc141910431246619746c4689e8548f6&view=mutil)
